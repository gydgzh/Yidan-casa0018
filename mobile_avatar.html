VEIL

Be together,
stay private.

Your face stays on your phone — only your expressions travel to your friend through a 3D avatar of your choice. ~6 KB/s, no cloud.

3 steps to start:
1. Pick a name + a shared room code (agree with your friend)
2. Pick the avatar you want others to see you as
3. Tap the button → allow camera → wait for your friend to join the same room

⚠ Important: if your friend is also on this Mac, make sure each window picks a DIFFERENT avatar — otherwise both faces will look identical on screen.
Avatars load from CDN on first use (~5 sec), then cached.

Where to get a .glb URL:
• readyplayer.me/avatar — design your own face, click "Done", copy the .glb link
• hub.vroid.com — anime VRM avatars (paste a download link if VRM)
• TalkingHead avatars — more realistic GLB heads (right-click → copy raw URL)

Veil sends only abstract facial expression coefficients (52 floats × 24 Hz ≈ 6 KB/s). No video, no audio, no identifiable face data.
MediaPipe Face Landmarker (Apache‑2.0), Three.js (MIT), TalkingHead avatars by met4citizen (MIT), @pixiv/three-vrm (MIT), self-trained 1D-CNN expression classifier.
